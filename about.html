    A SIMPLE IDEA, MADE PRACTICAL

    Not another course.
    A real connection.

    SkillSwap helps learners list what they can teach and what they hope to learn, discover complementary partners, and coordinate an exchange together.

    Our current project is a front-end demonstration. Profile details, preferences, reviews, and requests stay in your browser; there is no account service or message delivery backend connected.
