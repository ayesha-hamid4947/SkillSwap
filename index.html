    GOOD QUESTIONS

    A few things you might
    be wondering.

    Ask us anything
    What exactly is a skill swap?

    It’s a peer-to-peer exchange: you teach something you know in return for learning something you’re curious about. You and your partner decide together how to make it work.

    Does it cost anything to join?

    SkillSwap is built around sharing time and knowledge, not money. This demo stores profile, request, and preference data in your browser and doesn’t connect to a payment service.

    What if I’m still a beginner?

    Beginners are very welcome. You don’t need to be an expert to share something useful—start with what you know and be clear about what you’re hoping to learn.
